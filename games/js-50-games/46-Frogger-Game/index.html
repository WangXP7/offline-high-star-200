How to play

Guide the frog from the bottom grass to empty home slots across the road and river. Classic fixed playfield — not endless.

  • Move: Arrow keys or WASD (one hop per press). Use the on-screen D-pad on mobile.
  • Road: Cars kill on contact.
  • River: Stay on logs or turtles. Water or leaving the screen means death. Riding objects carry you sideways.
  • Homes: Enter an empty slot from the river. Fill the required homes before time runs out.
  • Timer: Resets each life. Time-out costs a life. Zero lives or a failed run ends the game.
  • Hard: Diving turtles, a median snake, and a second faster round after filling all homes.
