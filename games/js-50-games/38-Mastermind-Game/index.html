How to play

Guess the secret color code in a limited number of tries.

  • Tap colors to fill your guess, then press Submit.
  • Black peg — correct color in the correct position.
  • White peg — correct color in the wrong position.
  • Easy: 4 pegs / 4 colors / 12 guesses. Medium: 4 / 6 / 10. Hard: 5 / 8 / 8.
