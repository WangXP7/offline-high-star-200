How to play

Turn off every light. Each press toggles that light and its orthogonal neighbors.

  • Tap or click a cell to toggle it plus up, down, left, and right.
  • Puzzles are always solvable. Try to finish in fewer moves.
  • Easy 3×3 · Medium 5×5 · Hard 7×7.
